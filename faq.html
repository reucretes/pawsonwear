SIZING & FIT

Q: How do I measure my dog accurately?

Measure the chest (widest part behind front legs) and neck circumference tightly with a soft tape measure. Always add 1-2 cm of breathing room for comfort!

Q: What if my dog is between sizes?

We recommend sizing UP for heavyweight jackets and hoodies to ensure full range of motion. For stretch sleeveless tanks, size DOWN if you prefer a snug fit.
